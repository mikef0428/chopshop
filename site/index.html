Questions

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your stream, and you can post, edit or monetize them however you like. We never post them anywhere without asking you first.

How long does it take?

Within 24 hours of sending your link. Usually you send it before bed and the clips are there in the morning.

Which platforms do you take?

YouTube and Twitch VOD links. The clips come back vertical, sized for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Can my clips still get claimed?

Yes, they can. If your stream had copyrighted music or a game cutscene in it, that can still trigger a claim when you upload the clip. We can't promise any clip is claim-free, so it's worth a look before you post.

What's the end tag?

Free clips finish with a short Chop Shop tag at the end. That's how the free clip pays for itself. Paid clips and subscriptions have no tag.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. In your first 90 days, if you're not happy, ask and you get your money back. No questions.

Who actually runs this?

One person and an AI. The AI watches your stream, finds the moments and cuts the clips. I run the service and answer your messages myself. If you want a person to review every clip before it reaches you, that's what the Managed tier adds. No agency, no team of editors, no call center.
